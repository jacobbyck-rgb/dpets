Diamond ◆ Rework Playbook

Refused Costco loads from Diamond Pet Foods, from first call to invoice. Built from Diamond's ticket workbook and Jacob's account notes.

New order? Speed is the job.
  1. cx (carrier): WH name, address, hours, check-in WN#. The driver is waiting, and Diamond's BOL promises an answer within 20 minutes.
  2. Warehouse: tell them what's coming (WN#, pallets, SKUs, paperwork).
  3. ADW: ticket and email next, the same day.

Why this account feels different

On a normal account a customer books space and we store or move their freight. On Diamond, nothing is planned in advance. A Costco depot refuses part of a Diamond load at the dock, usually for damaged bags, and the carrier gets stuck holding it. WarehouseNow finds a crossdock near that depot, the crossdock reworks the pallets, and we book a new delivery back into Costco through the Costco portal. Then we bill Diamond per pallet. The Costco PO is the main key, because Diamond, ADW and Costco all track the order by it. Keep the WarehouseNow PO next to it for our own systems.

Why it’s called reverse logistics

Normal (forward) logistics moves freight one way: Diamond’s plant → truck → Costco depot → store. Most WarehouseNow accounts sit in that path, booked ahead of time. Diamond is the opposite. Every order starts when freight comes back out of that path: a receiver refuses it, it’s damaged, or there’s too much of it.

Reverse logistics stepOn Diamond
Refusal / returnCostco (or Chewy) refuses pallets for damage, a shifted load or an overage. Sticker: "FRT REMAINING ON TRAILER"
RecoveryFind a warehouse near the depot to get it off the carrier’s truck fast (the 20-minute rule)
InspectionWH counts by SKU, takes photos, finds the bad bags
Refurbish / reworkGood bags restacked on clean pallets ($65/pallet rework)
DispositionRework and redeliver, dispose/donate (under 10 bags), or return to the plant (overages back to Ripon)
Re-entryNew Costco appointment, often consolidated under a master PO, back into the depot

That’s why nothing is planned ahead, more parties are involved (Diamond, Costco/ADW, the carrier, the WH, sometimes Chewy), the goal is recovering product value, and everything is tracked by PO.

Standing rules

  • Speed: cx (carrier) gets the WH within 20 min, then the WH knows what’s coming, then ADW.
  • Under 10 damaged bags: always dispose/donate. Don’t wait for Thomas. Tell the WH to go ahead and send the count by SKU.
  • FTL on the PO / BOL: normal order. Coverage + Ops run it.
  • Who pays: assume Diamond direct unless Thomas or Jan says otherwise.
  • Never change SKUs on a ticket. Escalate to management.
  • Same WH + same depot: consolidate onto one master PO before scheduling.

    Finding the warehouse in Diamond's Excel

    1. Take the first 3–4 digits of the Costco PO. That's the depot code (1710918504 = 171).
    2. Open the Costco Locations PO reference tab. Find the code and read City Go to WHS (171 → Sumner, WA).
    3. Open the Go to Warehouses tab. Find that city in City1. Its row gives the WH code, name, hours, address and contact.
    4. Read the set prices on the same row: $ (delivery), Rate_per_Pallet (rework / in-out), Crossdock_Storage_Rates and Internal_Notes.
    5. Enter those as the Warehouse Quote in the app. If the city has two rows (Monrovia / Monrovia2, Dallas / Dallas2), pick one and type its code on the ticket by hand.
    6. City not on the list? Source a WH like a normal load, then add it to Go to Warehouses.

      Delivery appointments

      Reworked pallets go back into Costco on an appointment you book in the Costco appointment portal. On Master PO depots like Frederick and Mira Loma you book the Master PO, not each refused PO. You then email ADW the Master PO with the mini POs that roll into it, so they can match pallets to POs. When the portal flips to APPROVED, reply on the same thread with the confirmation.

      Consolidating POs onto a master PO

      Before you schedule, make sure every held PO at the same warehouse going to the same depot rides on one master PO. One truck, one appointment.

      1. 1See what’s held together. On the Order board, check Held at warehouse and the Consolidations list. Give POs at the same WH and depot the same Ships with name.
      2. 2Confirm with the WH that every pallet is reworked and ready, with counts by SKU.
      3. 3ADW creates the master PO (Frederick and Mira Loma weekly; others on request, e.g. Krista for Monroe Township). ADW may ask whether another held PO can ride along. Check the board and answer with the full list.
      4. 4Send the list in the team format using Copy master PO list on the board:
        Master PO: 9600930238
        WN-12156 // 1 PALLET // SKU 1162890
        WN-12681 // 1 PALLET // SKU 52296
      5. 5Request one appointment under the master PO in the portal, with the total pallet count. Put the master PO # in each order’s Master PO field.
      6. 6When approved, reply with the confirmation # and send the WH and carrier every WN# and PO on the truck.

      Example: WN-13087 (31 plts, PO 1750917236) + WN-12884 (1 plt 25350, PO 1750914355), both at Chem-Secure, on Krista’s master PO for Monroe Township.

      Weekly rhythm

      New refusals show up any day. On top of that, Jan Tappel set a fixed weekly cycle for depots that run on Master POs: rework and hold for up to a week, then request one Master PO every Wednesday.

      Today

      Jan's rule, in her words: "Rework and hold the refused product for one week and once a week on Wednesday, review the refused pallets and po numbers and request master po's for whatever was refused during the one week period." Mira Loma, CA uses Master POs. New Jersey and Texas don't always.
