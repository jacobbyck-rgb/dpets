Diamond ◆ Rework Playbook

Refused Costco loads from Diamond Pet Foods, from first call to invoice. Built from Diamond's ticket workbook and Jacob's account notes.

Why this account feels different

On a normal account a customer books space and we store or move their freight. On Diamond, nothing is planned in advance. A Costco depot refuses part of a Diamond load at the dock, usually for damaged bags, and the carrier gets stuck holding it. WarehouseNow finds a crossdock near that depot, the crossdock reworks the pallets, and we book a new delivery back into Costco through the Costco portal. Then we bill Diamond per pallet. The Costco PO is the main key, because Diamond, ADW and Costco all track the order by it. Keep the WarehouseNow PO next to it for our own systems.

The lifecycle of one refused load

Refusal comes in

  • Carrier reports a load refused at a Costco depot
  • #qb-team opens the Slack channel / AT order
  • Get the BOL, the receiver rejection sticker (mandatory) and pallet photos

Carrier → QB team

Decide disposition

  • Rework (most loads)
  • Donate / Dispose (small qty)
  • Return to the Diamond plant (overage)

Diamond SOP, Thomas Stone on exceptions

Line up the warehouse

  • Look up the go-to crossdock for that depot
  • Send the carrier WH name, address, hours, check-in #
  • Ask for driver info + ETA
  • Email the WH with paperwork + photos, pin the subject in Slack

You → carrier + WH

Ticket to ADW

  • New Ticket in Diamond's sheet
  • Depot code, contact and subject line fill in from the Costco PO
  • Send the email draft to the ADW contact for that depot

You → ADW + diamondosd@

Rework at the WH

  • Arrival photos + rework timing
  • Reworked pallet photos
  • Damage count in bags by SKU / Item ID, broken out by color if mixed

WH → you → Slack

Book the appointment

  • Pallets ready, WH + carrier available
  • Request in the Costco portal, 2+ days out
  • Wait for APPROVED or APPT REVISED
  • Send confirmation #, date/time and exact PO#s

You → Costco portal

Deliver + POD

  • Get the POD with the receiving sticker listing every PO# (mandatory)
  • podrequests@costco.com for receiving history
  • POD status → Completed

Carrier/WH → Costco

Invoice Diamond

  • Handling $25/plt, rework $65/plt
  • Redelivery $400, OT/weekend $200
  • Status → Invoiced

You → Ticket System

    Weekly rhythm

    New refusals show up any day. On top of that, Jan Tappel set a fixed weekly cycle for depots that run on Master POs: rework and hold for up to a week, then request one Master PO every Wednesday.

    Mon

    Intake

    • Refused product arrives at WHs
    • Unload + rework
    • Log every WN# and Costco PO
    Tue

    Intake

    • Same as Monday
    • Chase reworked-pallet photos
    • Confirm bag counts by SKU
    Wed

    Master PO day

    • List the refused pallets held this week
    • Email Diamond the WN#/PO pairs
    • Ask for a Master PO covering them
    • Then book the delivery
    Thu

    Appointments

    • Request the Master PO appointment in the portal
    • Follow up on pending approvals
    Fri

    Close out

    • Chase PODs
    • Move Delivered → Invoiced
    • Update the takeover tracker

    Wednesday email

    Subject: Master PO request – refused Diamond pallets week of [date] Hi Jan, Per the weekly process, here are the refused POs we reworked and are holding this week: WN-123 – Costco PO XXXXXXXXXX – [pallets] WN-456 – Costco PO XXXXXXXXXX – [pallets] Can we get a Master PO for these so we can schedule delivery? Thanks, Jacob

      Glossary

      cx
      The customer. On Diamond loads that's usually the carrier
      OS&D
      Over, Short & Damaged
      SU
      Single units (loose bags) instead of full pallets, e.g. "3 SU"
      ADW / ADWW
      The Costco-side reps for each depot. They receive the ticket
      Master PO
      One Costco PO covering several refused POs, so they deliver and invoice together
      POD
      Proof of delivery. Must show the receiving sticker with every PO#
      Depot code
      First 3–4 digits of a Costco PO, e.g. 10520914317 is Frederick
      Rejection sticker
      Costco's receiver sticker on refused freight. Mandatory from the carrier
      PO refresh
      Ask ADW to reissue a PO after a portal error or a missed appointment
