1 · 5 min

The account in 5 minutes

A Costco depot (sometimes Chewy) refuses part of a Diamond load, usually damaged bags or a shifted load. The carrier is stuck holding it. We find a crossdock near the depot, get the driver unloaded, have the WH rework the pallets, then book the redelivery back into Costco. We bill Diamond per pallet. Nothing is planned ahead: every order starts with a refusal.

Refusal→ WH address to carrier→ ADW ticket→ Rework + count→ PO refresh / master PO→ Costco appt→ Deliver + POD→ Quotes final → Mariah invoices

The key is the PO

Diamond, ADW and Costco all track by the Costco PO. We track by WN#. Always keep both side by side, in every email and on every label.

Speed is the job

Diamond's BOL promises the carrier an answer in 20 minutes. Order: carrier gets the WH address first, then the WH hears what's coming, then ADW gets the ticket the same day.

Thin margin on purpose

$400 deliveries are hard to hit; the rework spread covers it. We keep the account for the name and the Costco / Sam's / Chewy visibility. Every new carrier or contact is a lead.

Quotes, billing, invoices

What Diamond gets billed

Handled (in + out), every pallet offloaded$25 / plt Reworked (push & wrap counts as rework)$65 / plt Redelivery, once per truck$400 Example: 3 offloaded, 3 reworked, 1 truck$670

The app still shows $12.50 in + $12.50 out; Mariah merges them into one $25 line. Weekends / redelivery extras are added by hand.

Who does what

  • Me: keep WH + customer quotes in the app matching what really happened (pallet counts, rework, delivery, storage).
  • Mariah: invoices once the order is Complete and has a POD. Diamond-paid invoices go to Jan directly, on a separate chain.
  • Carrier pays: invoice goes to the carrier on the order thread; change the app Load # to the carrier.
  • Runners: $135 rework minimum on 1–2 pallet jobs; 1 week free storage.
  • Detention: we pay only if we took 2+ hours to give the address and get the driver offloaded, or failed to give a WH.

7 · 5 min

Rules cheat sheet

Carriers + warehouses

  1. Carrier gets the WH address within 20 min. Note how fast we gave it.
  2. Carriers send driver info / ETAs / docs to us. Never give a carrier the WH emails.
  3. Extra stop / out-of-route miles to drop at our WH is between the carrier and Diamond.
  4. Check WH hours before quoting them (Runners 0800–1700 M–F).

ADW + Costco

  1. Ticket to the depot's ADW rep same day, cc diamondosd@ + diamondpet@.
  2. Attach the portal screenshot when telling ADW an appt was requested / approved.
  3. Set the real delivery date before Request Appointment.
  4. Never change SKUs on a ticket. Escalate.
  5. Single damaged bag returned at the receiver: Anna's SOP template, no WN#.

Emails

  1. New thread in Front: Compose, tag the WN#, Diamond Pet mailbox.
  2. Every draft: To / Cc / Subject, then body, end with "Thank you," (Front adds the signature).
  3. Always list PO #s next to WN #s.
  4. Before saying something wasn't done, search Front, Slack and the board. Say "I can't find it."
